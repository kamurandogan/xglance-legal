Subscription

What does xGlance Pro include?

Only one thing: using the app without ads. Every statistic is available in the free version too.

How do I cancel?

  • iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → xGlance → Cancel Subscription.
  • Android: Google Play → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → xGlance → Cancel.

Pro stays active until the end of the period you already paid for. We cannot cancel a subscription for you.

I bought Pro but still see ads

Open More and tap Restore purchases, using the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with. If it still does not work, email us with your device type and purchase date.

Refunds

Purchases are processed by Apple or Google, so refunds are requested from them: Apple · Google Play.

Ads and privacy

How do I change my ad consent?

Open More → Privacy choices (shown where consent is required). On iPhone, tracking can also be turned off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Statistics

What do "78% · 14/18" and the "Low sample" badge mean?

78% is how often something happened; 14/18 means it happened in 14 of the 18 matches counted. "Low sample" means only a few matches were counted, so the number is less reliable.

I found a wrong score or statistic

Email us with the match, the screen and what looks wrong. Data comes from a third-party provider and is sometimes corrected after a match.

Does xGlance give betting tips?

No. xGlance only describes what happened in past matches. It gives no tips or predictions and offers no betting. Past results do not guarantee future ones.
