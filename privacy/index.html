Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the xGlance app ("xGlance", "we", "us") processes, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. xGlance shows descriptive football statistics. It has no user accounts: we never ask for your name, email address, phone number or location.

1. Who we are

xGlance is developed and operated by Kamuran Doğan, an independent developer, who is the controller of the personal data described here. For any question about this policy or your data, contact kamuran.software@gmail.com.

2. Information stored only on your device

  • Your favorite teams, app settings (language, theme) and the date you confirmed you are 18 or older.
  • Recently loaded statistics, kept as a cache so the app opens quickly.
  • Simple counters used to space out full-screen ads (for example, when the last one was shown).

This information never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

3. Information processed by service providers

Advertising (Google AdMob)

  • The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google may process your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android), IP address, device and app information, and your interactions with ads.
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland we ask for your consent through Google's consent message before personalized ads are used. If you do not consent, Google may still show limited, non-personalized ads.
  • On iOS, the advertising identifier is used only if you allow tracking in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • xGlance Pro subscribers see no ads, and the ad SDK is not started for them.
  • Gambling and betting ad categories are blocked in our ad settings.

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

  • We record how the app is used, for example which screens are opened, which statistics tab is viewed and whether a purchase screen was shown, together with an app-instance identifier and basic device information (model, operating system, app version, country derived from IP).
  • Analytics events never contain your name, email address, precise location or free text (for search, we record only the length of the query and the number of results).
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, analytics and ad storage follow the choices you make in the consent message.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

  • If the app crashes or hits an error, a report with the stack trace, device model, operating system version, app version and a crash-reporting installation identifier is sent so we can fix the problem.

Subscriptions (Apple, Google Play and RevenueCat)

  • The optional xGlance Pro subscription is sold by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never receive your payment details.
  • RevenueCat manages subscription status using a random, anonymous app user ID together with the store's purchase and transaction information. It is not linked to your name or email address.

Statistics server and team logos

  • The app downloads statistics from our server hosted by Supabase in the European Union (Ireland). Like any web server, it receives your IP address and standard request information, which may appear in short-lived technical logs. We do not use them to identify you.
  • Team and league logos are loaded from the content delivery network of our football data provider, Sportmonks (cdn.sportmonks.com), which receives your IP address and standard request information when an image is loaded.

Emails you send us

  • If you contact us by email (for example with "Send feedback" in the app), we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply.

4. Why we process data (legal bases)

  • Consent — personalized ads, and ad and analytics storage where consent is required (EEA, UK, Switzerland), and tracking on iOS. You can withdraw consent at any time (see section 7).
  • Contract — providing the subscription you purchased.
  • Legitimate interests — keeping the app working and secure, fixing crashes, understanding usage in aggregate, and showing non-personalized ads that fund the free app.
We do not sell your personal data, and we do not build profiles about you.

6. Retention

Data on your device stays until you delete the app. Analytics, crash and advertising data are kept by the providers above for the periods set in their services and our settings, and then deleted or anonymized. Emails are kept only as long as needed to handle your request.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Ad consent: in the app, open More → Privacy choices to review or change your consent (shown where consent is required).
  • Tracking on iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in your device settings.
  • No ads at all: xGlance Pro removes every ad.
  • Delete your data: see Deleting your data.
  • You may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and to data portability. Because xGlance has no accounts, we usually cannot link data held by providers to you; contact us and we will help where we can. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Deleting your data

xGlance has no user accounts, so there is no profile to delete. We do not keep a personal profile linked to your name, email address or phone number. What exists, and how to remove it, is below.

Data on your device

  • Your favorite teams, settings (language, theme), your 18+ confirmation, cached statistics and ad counters are stored only on your device.
  • Remove a favorite team in the app at any time. To delete everything, uninstall the app or clear its storage (Android: Settings → Apps → xGlance → Storage → Clear data).

Data held by service providers

  • Advertising (Google AdMob): reset or delete your advertising identifier in your device settings (iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID). xGlance Pro shows no ads and does not start the ad SDK.
  • Analytics and crash reports (Firebase): tied to a random app-instance identifier, not to your name or email. Uninstalling the app stops new data. Data already collected is kept by the provider according to its settings and then deleted or anonymized.
  • Subscription record (RevenueCat): RevenueCat stores your purchase status under a random, anonymous app user ID that the app does not show, so we find your record through your store order number (below).

How to request deletion

Email kamuran.software@gmail.com with the subject "xGlance data deletion" and include your store order number:

  • App Store: the Order ID in your Apple receipt email.
  • Google Play: the order number starting with GPA. in your Google Play receipt.

If you never subscribed, there is no purchase record and you do not need to contact us; the steps above are enough. We reply within 30 days and delete the matching records at RevenueCat and, where we can identify them, in our Firebase project. Records that Apple, Google or a payment provider must keep (for example purchase receipts and tax records) stay with them under their own policies, and we cannot delete them.

Subscriptions

Deleting your data or the app does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in the store: App Store (Settings → your name → Subscriptions) or Google Play (Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions). We cannot cancel it for you.

9. Age requirement

xGlance is intended for adults aged 18 or older and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly process data of anyone under 18.

10. Security

All connections use encryption (HTTPS). The app reads public statistics only and holds no account data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we use reasonable safeguards.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we change the "Last updated" date above. Significant changes may also be announced in the app.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email kamuran.software@gmail.com.
